A Private Note to Parents

When a brief checkup says “let’s just wait and see,” the quiet questions usually begin at bedtime.

Almost every thoughtful parent knows the feeling. You leave a ten-minute annual physical with a hasty percentile scribbled on a visit summary, yet at home you notice soccer teammates pulling half a head taller since last spring—or you catch yourself wondering at ten o’clock at night whether late homework lamps, selective dinners, or an earlier wrist X-ray mean your child’s open growth window is quietly narrowing.

We built KISFIT Atelier because families deserve something calmer and more honest than noisy supplement ads on one side or a six-month wait for a specialist clinic on the other. Childhood stature is not decided by a single Tuesday afternoon measurement; it is shaped by the relationship between chronological tempo, inherited family height, deep nocturnal sleep, and remaining skeletal runway.

Across five unhurried steps, this studio cross-references your child’s day-exact age against public US CDC 2000 LMS reference curves, parental genetic mid-height (MPH), evening sleep and posture habits, and—whenever a hospital wrist X-ray exists—elapsed time-lag (Δt) calibration between the date that radiograph was taken and today.

Your child’s current US CDC peer percentile position opens free by email, with two straightforward passes available afterward if you wish to keep the full Δt-calibrated adult height forecast, priority growth-bottleneck report, printable 30-day checklist PDF, or six-month monthly velocity tracker.
