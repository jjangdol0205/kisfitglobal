Parent Questions Answered

What is the average height for a 12-year-old girl in feet and inches?

On the public US CDC 2000 growth chart, the 50th percentile (median) for a 12-year-old girl is 4' 11.5" (151.2 cm). Healthy children commonly track anywhere from the 10th (4' 8.1") to the 90th (5' 3.0") percentile depending on family height.

How fast should a 12-year-old girl grow in a year?

Most pre-pubertal school-age children grow about 2.0 to 2.5 inches (5 to 6.5 cm) per year, speeding up during puberty before tapering in late adolescence. A routine pediatric checkup can verify your child's own curve.
