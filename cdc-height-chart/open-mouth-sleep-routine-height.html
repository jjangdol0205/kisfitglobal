Parent Questions Answered

Why do you mention mouth breathing during sleep?

Persistent nasal congestion or habitual mouth breathing can fragment deep slow-wave sleep and leave kids tired by morning. Mentioning chronic snoring or mouth breathing to your pediatrician is a helpful routine question.
